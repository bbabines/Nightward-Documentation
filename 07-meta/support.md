# Support

## How to get help

A community channel plus best-effort email, no per-ticket SLA:

- **Discord:** https://discord.gg/rTwHxcGnHg — the `#support` channel.
- **Email:** the seller contact on the Fab listing.

## Start with a support bundle

Before you write up a request, ask your agent to run `export_support_bundle`. It assembles a
redacted, local-only diagnostic zip — call log, a mutation slice, a log tail, version info, and a
latency snapshot. Nothing is transmitted automatically; you attach it yourself. It's the fastest
way to a real answer, since it removes the round-trip of "can you send me your setup?"

Most first-run issues are covered in **Troubleshooting** before you need to ask at all.

## No SLA, on purpose

A solo-scale operation can't honor a per-ticket SLA, and we won't sell a commitment we can't keep.
Support runs as best-effort — Discord and email, a human on the other end, no promised response
time. If a hard SLA is a purchase requirement, we're not the right buy today.

## Refunds and licensing questions

Refunds follow Fab's standard refund policy. For licensing questions (what a purchase covers,
seat/tier questions), see **FAQ** or ask in Discord.

## Full C++ source means you're never fully blocked

Every tier ships full C++ source. You can inspect a problem yourself while waiting on a reply — you
are never blocked purely on us for a fix you can read.
